Carrom board

Opponent: Robot

PLAYER 1
Your turn

Drag the striker backwards and release to shoot.

How to play: pocket your colour coins. The queen is worth extra, but pocket one of your own coins on the same turn to cover it. The striker is placed along your baseline. Drag away from the direction you want to shoot, then release. This is a simplified casual carrom ruleset.
