How to play FABCO

Move around the arena and collect glowing energy orbs. Avoid the storm as the safe zone gets smaller. Use your dash to escape danger and outlast the computer-controlled rivals.

Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move • Shift to dash.

Phone: Use the direction buttons and DASH button below the arena.

There are no firearms or realistic weapon mechanics in this prototype. The challenge is movement, timing, survival and strategy.
