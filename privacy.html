FABCO Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy describes the information handled by FABCO, a browser-based game prototype. Features and providers may change as the project develops. This page is a plain-language description of the current implementation, not a promise that every planned feature is already available.

1. Information FABCO handles

  • Guest play and device storage: game progress, settings, cosmetic selections and other local preferences may be stored in your browser or device using local storage.
  • When you sign in: the selected identity provider or email sign-in service processes the information needed to authenticate you. FABCO's authentication service (Supabase) receives account identifiers and may receive your email address and basic profile information made available by the provider, such as display name and profile image.
  • Cloud profile backup: if you sign in, FABCO may store your user ID, display name, avatar URL, game level, XP/coins and practice statistics, achievements, and cosmetic inventory/loadout in the FABCO profile database.
  • Online rooms: when multiplayer features are used, room participants may receive gameplay presence and player-state information such as nickname, position, appearance/colour and pings needed for the session.

2. How information is used

Information is used to sign you in, maintain your player profile, back up and restore game progress, operate online rooms, and troubleshoot service errors. The current prototype does not use cloud profile data to determine official competitive rankings or verified rewards.

3. Service providers

FABCO currently uses Supabase for authentication, database storage and realtime features. When you choose a third-party sign-in option, that provider processes your sign-in according to its own privacy policy. Supabase and the identity provider may process information on servers in different locations and under their own terms. Review their policies before signing in.

4. Cookies, local storage, advertising and payments

The current game uses browser local storage for game state and preferences. This prototype does not intentionally use advertising trackers, sell personal information, or offer real-money purchases. Hosting, security and authentication providers may use essential technical mechanisms to deliver their services.

5. Sharing and visibility

FABCO does not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. Information shared in an online room may be visible to other participants in that room.

6. Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you clear the site's data or remove it through your browser. Cloud profile information may remain in your account until it is deleted. The current prototype does not yet provide an in-game account-deletion control. To ask about access, correction or deletion of a cloud profile, contact anjik8893@gmail.com from the account email and include only the information needed to identify the request. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes. Authentication records may be retained by Supabase or the identity provider under their own retention rules.

7. Children and family guidance

FABCO is an early-stage project and is not intended to bypass age requirements or parental-consent rules. Users should follow the age requirements that apply to their location and to each sign-in provider. If you are a child or teen, involve a parent or guardian before creating an account or sharing personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information can contact us to request review and deletion.

8. Security and limitations

FABCO uses Supabase authentication and database access controls for the current account prototype. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. The multiplayer and progression systems are still prototypes; do not use FABCO to store sensitive information, and do not treat game scores or balances as financial value.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as FABCO changes. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Questions or privacy requests: anjik8893@gmail.com.

Project status: FABCO is a prototype. Some sign-in providers and online features may not be enabled or available in every release. This policy should be reviewed by a parent/guardian and, before public launch, by a qualified legal professional for the jurisdictions where FABCO will be offered.
